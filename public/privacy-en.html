Microphone and Pitch Analysis

With microphone permission, the app reads audio while in use and analyzes pitch, intonation, and string position on your device. Live analysis temporarily uses audio segments; it does not save a full practice recording unless you use a recording feature.

The microphone may also capture nearby voices or other sounds. Consider your surroundings and obtain permission from people present before recording. You can revoke microphone permission in iOS Settings. Recording and live analysis cannot operate without that permission.

Recordings and Practice Records

Recordings are stored on your device. The second page also saves practice settings, pitch and string-analysis results, and corresponding display and timing records for review and troubleshooting.

Each of the first two pages keeps its most recent successfully saved take. A new take replaces the previous one only after saving successfully. Empty takes or saving failures keep the previous recording. See Help and Support for recording controls and storage limits.

Practice Statistics and Preferences

Language, tuning preferences, and practice statistics are stored on your device for future use. Statistics include practice times, notes, pitch deviations, and measured note durations.

The second page automatically collects valid note statistics while it is visible in the foreground with the microphone on. REC is optional; collecting statistics alone does not save a full recording. Progress is saved periodically and when leaving the page, stopping the microphone, or backgrounding. A local backup helps recover statistics; unreadable originals may be kept on this device for recovery.

If you have used personal learning, your device may also contain short recordings and correction records. These are used only for on-device corrections and validation and are not uploaded automatically.

Data Transfers and Sharing

The app has no user accounts, cloud pitch analysis, or automatic recording uploads. It does not automatically send recordings, pitch, string-analysis results, or practice statistics to the developer or advertising services, or use this practice data for tracking across apps or websites.

When you choose to share files, recipients receive the content you provide. External websites and email services handle relevant data under their own privacy policies.

Advertising and Privacy Choices

When advertising is enabled, the app uses Google AdMob to provide non-personalized ads. Advertising services may process IP addresses and approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad interactions, performance data, and crash reports for ad delivery, measurement, and service operation. Non-personalized ads may still use connection and device information.

The app does not request cross-app tracking authorization. It requests ads only after any required privacy choices. Where available, change your choices under Ad Privacy Choices in Settings. For Google’s data practices, see https://policies.google.com/privacy .

Information You Send to Support

Contact details, issue descriptions, recordings, or diagnostic information you choose to send us are used only to respond, investigate your reported issue, and follow up about it. Support emails do not automatically attach recordings. Please provide only what is needed.

We retain this information only while handling the issue and necessary follow-up, then delete it when it is no longer needed. We do not use it for advertising or public display. Any separate research or model-training use requires your additional consent.

Reply to the original support email to request deletion of information you provided. We will delete copies under our control on request. Deleting the app does not delete support correspondence. Email-provider backups and retention are governed by that provider’s policy.

Retention, Deletion, and Backups

On the second page, tap DELETE and confirm “Delete this page’s recording and fingerprints?” to stop recording, delete this page’s saved and active audio and related records, and clear visible fingerprints. The first page’s recording and accumulated statistics are kept. Clear statistics separately on Statistics; this also removes its local backups and retained unreadable statistics.

Versions with personal learning let you clear its short recordings and correction records in Settings. To remove all local data, including the first page’s recording or data from older versions, choose Delete App in iOS iPhone Storage settings. Offload App keeps your data.

Shared files and sent emails are not deleted with the app. Some local data may be included in iCloud or computer backups under your system settings. Manage backups separately; restoring one may restore old data. The app has no separate cloud synchronization service.

App Store Purchases

Apple’s App Store handles payments. The app does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password. It reads and verifies purchase records on your device to provide or restore Premium access.

Transaction information is not added to recordings or practice statistics or sent by the app to the developer or advertising services. Purchases, restores, and refunds do not delete practice data.

Policy Updates

We update this policy and the date above when features or data practices change.

Website hosting

When this website is published on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

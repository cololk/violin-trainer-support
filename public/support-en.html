No Input or Microphone Permission Denied

Find Violin Trainer in iOS Settings and enable microphone access. You can also change it under Privacy & Security, Microphone. Return to the app and try again.

If you use Bluetooth or an external audio device, check which device is connected and whether its microphone is available.

Resuming After Calls, Siri, or Audio Device Changes

Calls, Siri, and audio input changes may pause listening. If the microphone was on and iOS permits resumption, the app reconnects the input. If you see Tap mic to resume or Unable to start microphone, tap the microphone button to try again.

An audio interruption, input change, or backgrounding stops an active take and attempts to save the recorded portion. A first-page saving failure keeps the previous take. Resuming listening does not resume recording. Wait for saving to finish, then press REC to start a new take.

Disconnecting headphones stops playback first to prevent sound moving directly to the speaker. After changing a Bluetooth or USB audio device, check its connection and microphone availability if listening does not recover, then tap the microphone to retry.

After an iOS audio service reset, tap the microphone to start listening again. Turning the microphone off yourself keeps it off when returning to the app or changing devices.

Pitch or Fingerboard Dot Does Not Match

Tune all four open strings first. Play a steady sustained note in a quiet place and check that the app's tuning reference matches your instrument. Fast string changes, quiet notes, background sounds, and different input devices may affect results.

A gray dot with an ellipsis means the string position is still being confirmed. When reporting a string-position issue, include the actual string, note, and location in the take when possible. The same pitch can be played on different strings.

Automatic Intonation Statistics

Play on Intonation Analysis with the microphone on to automatically collect valid sustained notes confirmed by the live dot. REC is optional. REC, STOP, and deleting recordings do not clear accumulated statistics. Leaving the page, stopping the microphone, or backgrounding saves the practice session.

A gray waiting dot, a retained previous note, or an unconfirmed position is not a new sample. Brief sounds and invalid pitch may not form a statistics record. Statistics use the key selected while playing; choose the matching key and period on Statistics to review them, or clear them separately.

During practice, progress is saved about every 15 seconds, including the measured portion of an ongoing note without counting it twice. A crash may still lose progress since the last successful save. Statistics shows a notice if saving fails or a backup is used. Free device space and tap Retry Saving when needed. Clearing statistics also deletes its backups and retained unreadable originals.

Recording, Replacement, and Deletion

Each of the first two pages keeps its most recent successfully saved take. REC starts a new take directly. The new take replaces that page's previous take only after it stops and saves successfully. Creation failures, empty takes, or save failures keep the previous take.

On the first page, tap REC to record audio. The same button then shows elapsed time and a stop icon; tap it again to stop, or tap PLAY to save the current take before playing it. Wait for Saving to finish. PLAY uses the newly saved take on success; a failure keeps the previous take and shows an error.

On the second page, tap REC to start; elapsed time appears above the button. Tap the same button to stop, then PLAY after saving to listen. Pitch, string-analysis results, and related practice records are saved with the audio. Do not force-close the app while it is saving. Leaving the second page stops its recording; calls, backgrounding, or input changes may also stop a take early.

A successfully saved second-page take replaces previous recordings, including those from older versions. DELETE asks “Delete this page’s recording and fingerprints?” Confirming stops recording, removes this page’s saved and active audio and related records, and clears fingerprints. Cancel keeps the data. The first page’s recording and accumulated statistics are kept.

Each new recording on the first two pages is limited to 1 GiB and leaves at least 200 MiB of device storage available. The previous take remains during replacement, so extra space is needed. Recording stops when a limit is reached or available space cannot be checked; a validated playable portion may be saved and replace the previous take. Empty or failed saves keep the previous take. At 48 kHz mono, audio alone uses about 659 MiB per hour; second-page decision records use additional space. Free device space before recording again, or use DELETE on Intonation Analysis to remove that page’s recording.

Unfinished first-page temporary recordings are cleaned up when the app next opens or a new take begins. File sizes and available storage are checked only on your device to determine whether recording can continue; storage information is not added to recording records or sent to the developer.

Managing Practice Data

The second page currently keeps one take. Use its DELETE button and confirm to remove this page’s recording, related records, and visible fingerprints. A new successfully saved take replaces the first page’s recording. Clear intonation statistics separately on Statistics.

Versions with personal learning let you clear short recordings and correction records in Settings; up to 24 diagnostic records are retained. To delete all local data, including the first page’s recording or older-version data, choose Delete App in iOS rather than Offload App, which keeps data.

Manage system backups, shared files, and support emails separately. Reply to the original support email to request deletion of information sent to support. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Advertisements and Privacy Choices

When advertising is enabled, successfully loaded ads appear at the bottom of the page. The banner stays hidden when offline, when no ad is available, or when required privacy choices are incomplete. You can continue practicing, recording, and using statistics.

When available, use Settings > Ad Privacy Choices to change your choices. If ads are temporarily unavailable, retry in Settings; the privacy policy is also readable offline. A single Premium purchase includes ad removal and All-Key Intonation Analysis. Purchases and restores are verified through the App Store.

Reporting an Issue

Do not include passwords, payment details, or unrelated personal information. Check that a recording is suitable to share before providing it.

We retain information you provide to support only while handling the issue and necessary follow-up, then delete it when no longer needed. You can also reply to the original support email to request deletion. Separate research or model-training use requires your additional consent.

Purchases, restores and purchase help

In Settings > Premium, one permanent purchase includes both ad removal and All-Key Intonation Analysis. There is no subscription or separate feature purchase. Analyze existing and new statistics in all supported major and natural minor keys; live pitch detection, recording, and practice data collection remain free. Prices use the App Store local currency. Versions with advertising disabled already show no ads; your verified ad-free access also applies if advertising is enabled in a later version.

If a product or price cannot be loaded, retry loading purchases. Test prices are never substituted. Stop recording or playback and wait for saving to finish before purchasing or restoring.

Restore Purchases checks the current App Store account for Premium without charging again. A valid, verified Premium purchase restores both ad removal and All-Key Intonation Analysis together. Try restoring after reinstalling, changing devices, or when ownership cannot be verified. An empty result is explicitly reported.

Pending purchases remain locked until approved and then update automatically. Unverified transactions do not grant new access; try restoring before purchasing again. Verified access is not cleared by price-loading errors, purchase cancellation or a failed restore.

Confirmed refunds or sharing revocations update access and return restricted statistics to free C major, preserving all practice data. Offline access uses transactions StoreKit can verify; external refunds or account changes may require a connection before they become known.
